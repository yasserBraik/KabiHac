Riyadh Engineer Builds Products That Read Both Ways

Principal Product Engineer, Design Technology Lead and HCI researcher, designing products that read naturally in Arabic and English.

By The Editorial DeskRiyadh BureauFiled 16 Nov 2026Reading time: one coffee

Yara Saud Al-Mansouri builds digital products in Saudi Arabia and the United Kingdom, across health tech, fintech, education and mobility. She leads teams from the first research interview to launch, and colleagues note she still designs and writes code every week.

Her work spans a national telemedicine service, a fintech super-app, an open-source library used across the Arab world and two startups of her own. Her PhD in Human-Computer Interaction, focused on Arabic interfaces, produced a granted patent.

She now serves as Principal Product Engineer and Design Technology Lead at Faseela Ventures, where she sets the front-end and design-system direction for the whole product.
